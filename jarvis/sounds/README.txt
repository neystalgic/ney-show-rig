JARVIS SOUNDS
=============
Wake chime: Jarvis plays chime1_rise.wav (Michael picked it Nov 13).

YOUR OWN CHIME: export a sound from Ableton and drop it in this folder named
   my_chime.wav   (or .aif / .aiff / .mp3 / .m4a)
It is used automatically from the next wake word (no restart needed after the first time Claude restarts Jarvis).
Tips: under 0.6 s, short tail, peak around -12 dBFS (it plays through the Mac speakers / your headphones),
not too bass-heavy, starts instantly (no silence at the front).
