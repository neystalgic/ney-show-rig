REST MODE PLAN -- saving energy without thinking about it (Oct 27)
==================================================================
One command puts the whole rig to rest; another wakes it for a show.
Saying it:  Light Desk button  |  "Hey Siri, rest mode" / "Hey Siri, show mode"  |  automatic (below)


WHAT REST MODE DOES, DEVICE BY DEVICE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Device              Rest mode                                   Show mode (wake)
 ------------------  ------------------------------------------  ------------------------------------
 Moving heads (4)    DMX: light off, park pointing at the        Plugs on -> wait ~20 s (their reset
                     ceiling. Then their Hue Smart Plugs off     sweep) -> DMX takes over
                     10 s later (no standby hum, no fan)
 Laser bar           DMX: off (then its smart plug, if added)    Plug on -> DMX
 Hue bulbs + floor   Off -- or a dim "night glow" if you pick    The look you were in before
   lamp              that as your rest look
 Hue Hub             Stops streaming; keeps listening (tiny      Starts streaming
                     power) so it can wake everything
 TouchDesigner       Pauses its timeline -- the Mac's graphics   Plays again, picks up where it was
   (Mac)             chip goes quiet
 Zephyrus            Sleeps (the hub sends it a sleep message)   Wake-on-LAN from the Mac or the Pi
                                                                 (needs the Ethernet cable)
 Projector           Its own Eco mode + auto power-off (set in   Power button / remote
                     ITS menu). Never a smart plug.
 Mac, Scarlett       Never switched off by rest mode, never on   --
                     a smart plug. The Mac sleeps on its own.
 Studio monitors,    Manual switched power strip, flipped off    Flip it on
   synths, Elektrons at night (a plain switch, not smart)
 Raspberry Pi        Stays on (5-8 W, built for 24/7). It's      --
                     what notices if something's left on.


AUTOMATIC
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 - Show stops / movie ends  -> heads go to DMX sleep right away (lights off, parked).
 - 30 min of nothing (no sound on the Scarlett, no Push or MIDI, not in movie mode)
                            -> SOFT rest: heads asleep, TouchDesigner paused.
                               Nothing switches off at the wall automatically.
 - Full rest (smart plugs off, Zephyrus asleep) only when you ask for it -- or at a time
   you choose (e.g. 2 am), never in the middle of anything.
 - Your phone gets a quiet note from the Pi if something big is still running late at night.


SAFETY (unchanged)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 - Flicker guard stays on in every mode. Waking never flashes: everything fades up.
 - Heads park pointing UP before their power is cut, so the reset sweep on wake starts
   from a safe position.
 - Rest mode never cuts power to the Mac, Scarlett or projector.
