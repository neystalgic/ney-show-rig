Calibrate

  1. Tap Steer. The head glows dim blue and follows these sliders for 60 s.
  2. Move Tilt until the beam points straight up at the ceiling, then tap This is straight up.
  3. Set Max lean so the beam can't come down to where heads are (sitting or standing nearby).
  4. Optional: limit pan so it never sweeps toward the mirror or the glass door.
  5. Tap Calibrated. Done.
