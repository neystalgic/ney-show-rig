"HEY SIRI, NEY" -- talk to Jarvis from your iPhone, Apple Watch or Mac (about 3 minutes, once)
=====================================================================================
Say "Hey Siri, Ney", then say what you want in plain words:
  "purple lights" / "show mode" / "movie with the stars" / "goodnight" / "status" /
  "log 268.4" / "don't dim until midnight" / "schedule back to normal" /
  "purple and don't dim until 12:30"   (two things in one sentence works)
Siri speaks Jarvis's answer back.

BUILD IT (iPhone, Shortcuts app)
 1. Tap + (new shortcut). Tap the name at the top > Rename > Ney
 2. Add action: Dictate Text            (Stop Listening: After Pause)
 3. Add action: URL Encode              (it picks up Dictated Text automatically)
 4. Add action: Text   and type exactly:
      http://Michaels-MacBook-Pro-2.local:7040/ask?text=
    then, right after the "=", tap the variable bar and insert  URL Encoded Text
 5. Add action: Get Contents of URL     (input: Text)
    Tap the arrow to show more > Headers > Add new header:
      Key:   X-Token
      Value: the rig token -- open "rig_control.token" in this folder on the Mac
             (TextEdit), copy it, paste it here. Same key your other rig Shortcuts use.
             Never paste it anywhere else.
 6. Add action: Speak Text              (input: Contents of URL)
 7. Done. Say "Hey Siri, Ney". The first time, iOS asks to allow local network access: Allow.

ON THE MAC (same shortcut, synced through iCloud)
 - Shortcuts app > right-click "Ney" > Get Info / details > Add Keyboard Shortcut
   (try Control-Option-Space). Press it, speak, done.
 - Want to TYPE instead of talk on the Mac? Duplicate it as "Ney Type" and swap step 2
   for "Ask for Input" (Text).

APPLE WATCH: the iPhone shortcut shows up on the Watch automatically ("Hey Siri, Ney").

ONLY WORKS AT HOME (same Wi-Fi as the Mac). Away from home comes later (Tailscale).
If Siri says it can't connect: is the Mac awake? Try http://Michaels-MacBook-Pro-2.local:7040
in Safari on the phone -- "Not allowed." means the Mac is reachable (that's the token check).
