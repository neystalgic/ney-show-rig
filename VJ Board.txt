PUSH 2 VJ BOARD  --  64 sacred geometry recipes
================================================

Select the VJ track on Push. All 64 pads are now geometry recipes.
Tap a pad: its geometry BUILDS out from the centre (1 bar), melting out of whatever was
there before, then reaction diffusion GROWS out of its lines and slowly dissolves it
(over 4 bars). Tap the next pad and the new geometry builds out of the old one.

ROWS (bottom to top) = the sacred family
   row 1  Flower of Life
   row 2  Metatron's Cube
   row 3  Sri Yantra
   row 4  Golden angle (Fibonacci)
   row 5  Tesseract
   row 6  Chladni (sound patterns)
   row 7  Quasicrystal
   row 8  Mandala

COLUMNS (left to right) = how it is constructed
   1  the pure form
   2  six-fold symmetry
   3  seed of life: the form + 6 copies around it
   4  twelve-fold, close up
   5  flower of forms: 1 + 6 + 12 copies
   6  two eight-fold wheels turning against each other
   7  golden-ratio nest, five deep
   8  three-fold

THE 8 KNOBS ABOVE THE SCREEN = THE FREESTYLE RACK (after you've tapped a VJ pad;
they go back to Live as soon as you play a note on another track)
Every knob is an effect you turn IN: all the way left = off. They stack, they all ride the
beat clock, and they all go through the flicker protection.
   1 STROBE    white flashes on the grid: every bar > half bar > every beat
   2 PRISM     a rainbow across the room that rolls round every two bars
   3 ORBIT     a comet runs round the room. Centre = off (it clicks to a stop there),
               right = clockwise, left = counter-clockwise; further out = faster
   4 BREATHE   the room breathes in and out over two bars; past halfway it becomes a
               wave rolling lamp to lamp
   5 PUMP      sidechain: every beat ducks the lamps and they swell back (house pump)
   6 ECHO      every kick fires a pulse that bounces lamp to lamp, like a delay
   7 CHORD     the lamps split into the notes of the chord -- root, third, fifth (seventh) --
               each in its degree colour: what you hold, or the chord the brain hears
   8 SPARKLE   the hats throw glitter onto random lamps
   Push shows nothing for these; the Light Desk (localhost:7020) shows the values.

HOLD SHIFT + turn = THE CLASSIC KNOBS
   1 Brightness  2 Effect (wash > glow > pulse > chase > sparkle > sweep > hit)  3 Effect speed
   4 Spread      5 Beat punch      6 Fade (in beats)      7 Vibrance      8 Smoothing
   BPM SYNC: everything runs on the Digitakt's MIDI clock (Live's tempo). If something would
   flash faster than the Flicker Guard allows (now 2.8 a second, never more than 3), it halves
   -- still on the grid -- until it's safe.

CAPPED LAMPS: the Hue Play floor lamp and Hue color lamp 1 (by the projector) never go above
50%, so they don't wash out the picture.

COLOURS: every note -- the VJ pads themselves AND what you play on 1 MIDI -- colours the geometry and the lamps by its
step in the key (F minor now): 1 red 2 orange 3 yellow 4 green 5 blue 6 indigo 7 violet,
the 1 an octave up = rainbow.
KEY FOLLOWS LIVE: once AbletonOSC is picked as a Control Surface in Live, change the Scale
(key + scale) in Live's top bar and the colours follow within a second. Without it, the key
is set on the Light Desk (or /project1/vj, Key + Scale).

AUTOPILOT CONDUCTOR (on by default -- /project1/vj, "Autopilot conductor")
   When you're not tapping pads, the board plays itself: a new recipe every 8 bars,
   landing on the phrase, chosen to suit what the music is doing:
     groove     the five classic families in turn, constructions getting richer
     breakdown  calm, open forms (flower, golden angle, chladni), lots of diffusion
     drop       the dense ones (quasicrystal, mandala, metatron, tesseract), zoom-through
     quiet      holds still
   A drop or breakdown gets a new recipe right away. Tap any VJ pad and you're in charge:
   the autopilot waits 4 bars after your last pad ("Autopilot waits after you play"),
   then carries on from where you left it. Every 4 / 8 / 16 bars: "Autopilot: new recipe every".

WHY NOT PUSH'S BUTTONS: in Live mode every Push button also does something in Live
(the ones under the screen even change the selected track, which would knock you off
the VJ track). So the looks and transitions live in TouchDesigner, and the knobs run the lights.

IN TOUCHDESIGNER  (/project1/vj)
   Recipe / Family / Construction, Build time (bars), Diffuse + Diffuse time (bars),
   Build spin / scale, Look (clean/ASCII/8-bit/outline/superflat/mirror), Transition, plus the older decks (your geometry show, your mix, ...) on
   the Deck menu.  Takeover: 1 = VJ board on the projector, 0 = your own comp1/comp2 mix.
   Rebuild: Hue Rig/build_vj.py

ABLETON (press Cmd+S in Ableton to keep it)
   Settings > Tempo & MIDI: Output "Online IAC Driver (Bus 1)" Track ON, Input Track OFF
   1 MIDI        your Digitone track
   Note Colours  MIDI From 1-MIDI (Post Mixer) -> IAC ch 1, Monitor In
   VJ            MIDI track, no devices, MIDI To IAC ch 16 -- select it on Push for the 64 recipes
   Don't select Note Colours on Push (it's just a pipe). Don't use Solo while performing.

SAFETY: everything ends in the flicker-safe pass set by the hub's Flicker Guard
(now 2.8 flashes/s, hard limit 3). Nothing can flash faster than that.
