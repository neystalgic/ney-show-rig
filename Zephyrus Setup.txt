ZEPHYRUS SETUP  --  links + checklist, v2 (Oct 24)
ASUS Zephyrus, RTX 4070 laptop GPU (8 GB video memory)
==========================================================================
The idea: the Mac stays the show brain (Ableton, Hue Hub, VJ board, flicker-safe pass).
The Zephyrus is the 3D + AI render box. Everything it does is driven by the same MIDI
that already runs the show, plus what the hub now hears in the music (chords, key,
sections, drums), all sent to it as MIDI over the network. Its picture comes back to
the Mac (NDI) and goes through the flicker-safe pass like everything else.

WHAT'S NEW ON THE MAC SINCE v1 (already working, nothing to do)
   - Key follows Live: change Live's key/scale and the colours follow (AbletonOSC)
   - Audio brain: the hub hears chords + key in Ableton's master (Essentia)
   - Autopilot conductor: the VJ board picks recipes on its own by song section
   - Code is on GitHub (private): github.com/neystalgic/ney-show-rig

SHOW MIDI MAP  (one language for the whole rig -- everything listens to this)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Running today:
   ch 1    NOTES you play (Note Colours track)       pitch -> degree colour, velocity -> intensity
   ch 2    HEARD CHORDS (audio brain)                 the chord in the mix when you're not playing
   ch 16   VJ PADS (VJ track)                          which of the 64 recipes
   CLOCK   Digitakt MIDI clock                         beat + bar sync, start / stop
   KEY     from Live (AbletonOSC)                      what "1 = red" means right now
Being wired up (Digitakt MIDI tracks -> Scarlett MIDI In):
   ch 9 kick   ch 11 snare   ch 12 hats               exact drum hits
Added by the hub relay once rtpMIDI is up (the show's "state" as MIDI):
   ch 15  CC 1  degree colour (1-8)        CC 7  diffuse amount
          CC 2  geometry family (0-7)      CC 8  brightness / master
          CC 3  construction (0-7)         CC 9  look (clean, ASCII, 8-bit ...)
          CC 4  kick (envelope)            CC 10 lighting effect
          CC 5  loudness                   CC 11 song section (quiet/groove/breakdown/drop)
          CC 6  build progress             CC 12 key root (0-11)
   (audio is turned INTO MIDI on the Mac, so the Zephyrus never needs the audio itself)

What listens on the Zephyrus:
   Unreal:         ch16 = build that recipe's 3D geometry, ch1/ch2 = colour + light pulses,
                   clock = camera / tunnel speed on the beat, kick = shockwave,
                   CC11 section = camera move (calm drift in breakdowns, fly-through on drops)
   TouchDiffusion: CC2 family -> prompt style, CC1 degree -> palette words,
                   CC5 loudness -> how strongly it repaints, CC11 drop -> wilder prompts
