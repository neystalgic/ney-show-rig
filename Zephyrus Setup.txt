ZEPHYRUS SETUP  --  links + checklist (RTX 4070 laptop, 8 GB video memory)
==========================================================================
Idea: the Mac stays the show brain (Ableton, Hue Hub, VJ board, flicker-safe pass).
The Zephyrus is the 3D + AI render box. EVERYTHING it does is driven by the same MIDI
that already runs the show (IAC Bus 1 + the Digitakt clock), sent over the network.
Its picture comes back to the Mac over Ethernet (NDI) and through the safety pass.


STEP 0 -- BEFORE ANYTHING (Zephyrus, plugged in)
-------------------------------------------------
[ ] Windows Update (Settings > Windows Update), restart
[ ] NVIDIA Studio driver for "GeForce RTX 4070 Laptop GPU"
    https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/drivers/
[ ] Power: plugged in, Armoury Crate > Turbo / Performance mode
[ ] Check free disk space (want 150 GB+ free: Unreal + AI models)
[ ] Claude desktop app, sign in, link it to our conversation (so I can do the rest)
    https://claude.ai/download
[ ] Ethernet cable from the Zephyrus to the router (Wi-Fi works, wired is steadier)


STEP 1 -- THE MIDI BRIDGE (the heart of the plan)
--------------------------------------------------
Mac side (built in already, just turn on):
[ ] Audio MIDI Setup > Window > Show MIDI Studio > Network (globe icon)
    > "+" under My Sessions, name it "Show", tick Enabled
    > allow "Anyone" to connect (or add the Zephyrus once it appears)
Zephyrus side:
[ ] rtpMIDI (free, Windows network MIDI that talks to the Mac's session)
    https://www.tobias-erichsen.de/software/rtpmidi.html
    > open it, the Mac's "Show" session appears under Directory > Connect
I'll then make the Hue Hub relay the show MIDI into that session (see MIDI MAP below).

SHOW MIDI MAP  (one language for the whole rig -- everything listens to this)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Already running today:
   ch 1   NOTES you play (Note Colours track)   pitch -> degree colour, velocity -> intensity
   ch 16  VJ PADS (VJ track)                    which of the 64 recipes (row = family, column = construction)
   CLOCK  Digitakt / Ableton MIDI clock         beat + bar sync, start / stop
Added by the hub relay (so the Zephyrus gets the show's "state" as MIDI too):
   ch 15  CC 1  degree colour (1-8)        CC 6  build progress
          CC 2  geometry family (0-7)      CC 7  diffuse amount
          CC 3  construction (0-7)         CC 8  brightness / master
          CC 4  kick (envelope)            CC 9  look (clean, ASCII, 8-bit ...)
          CC 5  loudness                   CC 10 lighting effect
   (audio is turned INTO MIDI on the Mac, so the Zephyrus never needs the audio itself)

What listens on the Zephyrus:
   Unreal:        note ch16 = build that recipe's 3D geometry, ch1 notes = colour + light pulses,
                  clock = camera / tunnel speed on the beat, ch15 CCs = glow, fog, particles
   TouchDiffusion: ch15 CC2 family -> prompt style, CC1 degree -> palette words,
                  CC5 loudness -> how strongly it repaints
