The gear plays the lights

-
-
The music drives the brightness; the look, your pads and Ableton give the colours. Kick: the room punches to full and settles back. Snare: its lamps flip to the room's accent colour. Hats: one lamp at a time shimmers. Breathe: how much the room between hits follows the loudness. Colour moves: the colours step round the room every bar (a one-colour room goes two-tone). Drops: when the kick drops out the room goes low and pale; when it comes back, full brightness and colour, and the visuals burst. Sound colour (off by default): bassy = warmer, bright = cooler. No white strobes; everything still goes through the Flicker Guard.

The show

-
and on every drop
Programmed: like a lighting operator on the beat grid. The lamps move together or in clean steps, in two colours (from your look / pads) held for a whole phrase; Auto changes the cue every phrase and on drops; breakdowns go slow and soft. It starts once the hub has locked onto the tempo (a few seconds of music) -- until then it plays Reactive.
Ripples, waves, bounces and spins use where your lamps are (Setup > Move lamps); chases and colour moves use your order (Setup > Set the order). "Ripple travel" sets how long a hit takes to cross the room. When the hub has locked onto the tempo, the kicks land on the beat grid -- sent early so the bulbs light ON the beat. Each lamp still flashes no more than the Flicker Guard allows.

Movie mode

off
While Movie mode is on, the lamps take the colours of the picture around where they sit (left lamps the left of the picture, and so on), instead of the look, pads and music -- like an ambilight, with no sync box. Turn it off and the show comes back. The Flicker Guard still protects you from strobing scenes.
Capture card: HDMI splitter -> projector + a USB capture card into this Mac; on the PS5 turn off Settings > System > HDMI > Enable HDCP (games only -- Netflix and other video apps won't play with it off). This Mac's screen: macOS asks once to allow Screen Recording for TouchDesigner (protected streaming video shows black). Camera: any webcam aimed at the projection; trim its view in TouchDesigner (movie_in > camera_crop) and keep the lamps from shining on the screen.

Ableton plays the lights (IAC Driver)

-
Keep Push 2 in Live mode (scenes, clips, Expressive Chords) and send Live's MIDI here: 1. Mac: Audio MIDI Setup > Window > Show MIDI Studio > double-click IAC Driver > tick "Device is online". 2. Live: Settings > Link, Tempo & MIDI > Output "IAC Driver": Track on. 3. A MIDI track: MIDI From = the track you play, MIDI To = IAC Driver, Monitor = In (clips on it work too). Every note goes through the same pad data as User mode: colour, geometry, latch and flicker protection. User mode still works.

Colour match

Tap a test colour: every lamp and every Push pad show it, and the big swatch shows what you should be seeing. Start with white: if the lamps look too blue, green or pink, turn that channel down under Lamps; same for the Push under Push 2. Then check the other colours.

Timing: lamps, Push and TouchDesigner together

The bulbs show a colour a little after it's sent (the bridge passes it on over Zigbee 25 times a second). Push and TouchDesigner wait Lamp delay so all three change together. Press Sync test: lamps, every Push pad and the TouchDesigner visuals swap between red and blue once a second. Move Lamp delay until they swap at the same moment.
