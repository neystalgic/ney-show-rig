HEALTH REPORT v2 -- built + tested by the Rig away loop, Nov 14 2026. Not live yet.
=====================================================================================

WHAT IT DOES
The Body page (/body) now leads with your real daily DEFICIT:
  deficit = (Active kcal + Resting kcal from the Watch) - calories eaten (Food Log)
- New top row of tiles: latest deficit (green at 500+), 7-day average deficit, adaptive burn.
- The latest deficit is the newest day that has BOTH a food log and a Health Sync (usually yesterday).
- No Resting kcal from the Watch that day -> estimated with Mifflin-St Jeor (male, 190.5 cm,
  born ~1999, your latest weigh-in on or before that day) and labelled "(est.)"; faded bar on the chart.
- A day with a meal logged WITHOUT calories is marked * and left out of the averages.
- Adaptive burn (the truest maintenance number) appears once you have 14+ days of complete food
  logs and 14 days of weigh-ins: average eaten + (7-day-average weight drop per day x 3500),
  over up to the last 28 days. Until then the tile says how many more days it needs.
- New charts: daily deficit (bars + 7-day average line + dashed 500 goal), resting HR + HRV
  (HR left axis, HRV right axis), sleep hours (dashed 7 h guide).
- "Resting HR 57 bpm (11-13) vs 7-day average 60 (-3)" line under the heart chart.
- Weekly table gains Deficit, RHR, HRV and Sleep columns (scrolls sideways on the phone).
Same canvas style, purple accents, no libraries. Everything the old page used is unchanged
(the test proves the v1 numbers are identical), and Jarvis's add_food()/today() are untouched.

WATCH FOR
- Deficit tile shows "–" until a day has both a food log and a Health Sync. Normal.
- If most days say "(est.)", the Health Sync shortcut isn't sending resting_kcal -- add
  "Resting Energy" to the shortcut. The estimate is decent but the Watch number is better.
- Resting estimate uses age = this year - 1999 (approximate). Change BIRTH_YEAR / HEIGHT_CM /
  DEFICIT_GOAL at the top of the v2 section in body_log.py if needed.
- Adaptive burn is only as good as the food log: skipped meals make it read low.
