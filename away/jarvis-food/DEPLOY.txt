JARVIS FOOD LOGGING POLISH -- built + tested by the Rig away loop, Nov 14 2026. Not live yet.
=============================================================================================

WHAT IT DOES
- Fixes the Nov 13 bug: "I ate a Chipotle chicken quesadilla, about 1050 calories and 60 grams of
  protein" now logs "a chipotle chicken quesadilla", 1050 kcal, 60 g. Filler words (about, around,
  roughly, like, approximately, maybe...) and stray punctuation never end up in the item name.
  "1,050 calories" with a comma works too.
- Fractions scale the ESTIMATE: "half the chips and queso", "a third of a pizza", "two thirds of the
  burrito", "a quarter of the cake", "3/4 of my sandwich". Jarvis asks Ollama for the whole thing and
  logs that share. If you say the numbers yourself ("half a burger, 600 calories") they're logged
  exactly as said, never scaled. "a quarter pounder" / "half pound burger" are not fractions.
- "the extra chicken" logs as "extra chicken", estimated as one extra side portion.
- "the rest of X": Jarvis looks for the earlier part of X in today's/yesterday's Food Log (e.g. "half
  the chips and queso") and logs the remaining share, worked back from the calories you already
  logged -- no new guess, so the two rows add up to one whole. If it can't find the first part it
  counts half an estimated portion and says so.
- Several foods in one sentence become separate rows: "the extra chicken and half the chips and
  queso" -> 2 rows. A new food starts after a comma, or after and/plus/then when the next word sounds
  like a new portion (a, the, half, two, some, my, extra, a number). So "chips and queso", "mac and
  cheese", "rice and beans" stay one food. Numbers attach to the food they follow
  ("a burger 700 calories and a coke 140 calories").
- Reply for several foods: "Logged 2 things: extra chicken: 250 calories, 45 grams of protein (my
  estimate); and half the chips and queso: 600 calories... Today so far: ..."
- Small fix: "Jarvis, ..." with a comma no longer counts as a separate empty command when a
  sentence is split into pieces.
Everything else in jarvis.py is unchanged (all 27 phrases + every older test still pass).

WATCH FOR
- Splitting is word-rule based. Two foods joined by plain "and" with no portion word ("burger and
  fries") stay ONE row (estimated together -- usually fine). Say "a burger and some fries" to split.
- Mixing food and a lights command with a comma ("I ate chicken, and turn the lights purple") may
  log the command words as a food. Keep food sentences on their own for now.
- "the rest of X" matches by words, so say X the same way both times ("chips and queso").
- Item names are stored lower-case (as before).
