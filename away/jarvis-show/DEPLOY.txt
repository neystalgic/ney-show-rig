JARVIS SHOW PHRASES (away loop W3, Nov 17) -- built + tested offline, NOT live yet
==================================================================================

WHAT IT DOES
  Talk to the show director by voice (Siri "Jarvis" or typed). Word rules only -- the local model can't
  pick these, and nothing here strobes.
    "lock wave" / "lock the heads" / "hold gobos" / "lock call and response"   -> holds that act or mode
    "auto" / "unlock" / "back to auto"                                        -> director picks again
    "more audio" or "audio only" = 100% music   "clock only" / "just the drummer" = 0% (built-in drummer)
    "normal audio" / "half and half" = 60% (default)   "less audio" = 30%   "audio at 75 percent"
    "wave backwards / forwards / bounce / alternating wave"
    "loop clockwise / counter clockwise / alternate the loop"
    "acts off" / "modes only"   and   "acts on"
    "what's playing"  -> "Playing Gobos, bar 3 of 8, groove. Auto picks the next one."
  Two in one works: "acts off and loop clockwise".
  "lock burst" is refused by voice (the drop flashes on the kick) -- it still plays in drops, or use the console.
  Hub off? The setting is saved for the next show (same as the console / POST /show/set does).
  NOTE: the backlog listed "audio only" as 0.6; the hub's own meaning is 1 = audio only, so "audio only" = 1.0
  and 0.6 is "normal audio". Say if you want it the other way.
