RIG CONSOLE v1 -- DEPLOY  (away loop, Nov 17 -- backlog W2)
=========================================================

WHAT IT DOES
A phone page for doing the show by hand: http://<Mac>:7040/console  (same login as /health -- open it once with
?token=... and the phone remembers the key in a cookie, exactly like /health and /hub).
- Top: what's playing right now, every 1 s -- act or zone mode, bar x of 8 (an 8-step strip), section, tempo, audio
  level meter, current look, guest mode.  Real data only (the hub's /api/live show status); "Hub off" when it's off.
- Director: "Show modes on" (dmx_pattern modes) / "Heads together" (normal).
- Acts: Auto (unlock) + the 6 acts -- tap = lock.  Zone modes: the 7 modes -- tap = lock.
  Purple fill = locked, purple underline = what's playing now.
- Wave direction (bounce/forward/back/alternate), loop direction (alternate/CCW/CW), audio mix slider
  (clock only <-> audio only, sends on release), head speed 0.5x-2x, acts on / modes only.
- Quick looks: purple, cozy, movie chill, night, heads on/off, guest on/off.  Heads off + Guest off need a 2nd tap
  within 3 s.
- Zone map 1A 1B 2A 2B 3A (1 = front door) with the heads in each zone; a zone glows purple with its heads' level.
Design: dark stage background, one purple accent, no gradients/glass/emoji, 44 px buttons, phone-first.
It only uses routes that already exist: POST /show/set (its allowlist is unchanged -- strobe/safety keys are not
reachable) and the looks.  When the hub is off, /show/set saves the settings for the next show (as before).

WATCH FOR
- The header says "live hh:mm:ss" and ticks each second.  "Not signed in" = open the ?token= link once on that phone.
- Tap a mode with the show running: it should go solid purple, and within a phrase the underline moves to it.
  Auto = back to the director's choice.
- Hub off: the top says "Hub off"; taps still answer "Saved for the next show".
- Wave/loop direction lights come from hub_state.json (the hub saves settings there), so after a tap they can lag a
  second or two until the hub writes it.
- The page polls /console.json every second while it's open (one tiny local request to the hub each time).  Close
  the tab if you ever see the hub struggle.
- Spot power (W1) and spot-calib (W7) are separate and unchanged by this.
