WHAT IT DOES
A dry-run harness that "presses" the Tap Switch Mini's buttons 1-4 against a FAKE bridge (no switch, no
lights, no network) and checks each one lands on the right look. It already ran against your live
tap_switch.py and found two things worth fixing before the switch arrives (~Tue Nov 17):

  Button layout -- all correct:  1 -> show   2 -> movieuv   3 -> cycle (rainbow, flash, hype, movieglow,
  cozy, then round again)   4 -> night.  Every one of those is a real look in rig_control.LOOKS.

  BUG 1 (the real one): HOLDING a button fired it TWICE (once on press, again on Hue's "long_press").
    Button 3 skipped a look on every hold; buttons 1/2/4 re-ran their look twice in a row.
  BUG 2 (small): a very first quick tap of a never-pressed button could be swallowed if it fell
    between two polls.
  Fixed copy: away/tap-test/tap_switch.py -- one press = one action, however you press it.
  Nothing else changed (same layout, same cycle, same functions, still only READS the bridge).
